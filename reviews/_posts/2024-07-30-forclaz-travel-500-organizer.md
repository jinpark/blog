> **TL;DR**
> 
> Not too expensive travel backpack with built in organization for up to a week long trip. Recommended if you need a bigger travel bag.

---

This [backpack](
https://www.decathlon.com/products/forclaz-travel-500-organizer-40-l-backpack-338564?variant=41023932366910) is a cheaper alternative to a lot of more expensive travel backpacks out in the market. It has one big zippered compartment in the back half, and two smaller compartments on the front half of the bag. The front half is meant to keep shoes in one of the compartments but it definitely does not fit any of my shoes (US11.5/285). Theres a padded laptop compartment in the back but it is not a floating compartment. The compartments allow you to not have to use packing cubes for organization. 


![backpack-inside]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/images/forclaz-front.webp)[^1]

The back has two well padded shoulder straps and a waist strap. Sadly, the waist straps cannot be stowed away. Instead, you can flip them around and hook them the opposite way toward the backpack to keep them from flopping around when not in use. Theres a lot of padding for the waist which makes it comfortable to have on your back and prevents the bag from hitting your waist when walking around.

![backpack-back]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/images/forclaz-front.webp)[^1]

This backpack is a "homage" to the much more expensive Cotopaxi Allpa (35/42). The Allpa has a bit more thought put into the design. The waist straps can actually be stashed normally. Theres a luggage passthrough in the back. But the laptop pocket is still not a floating design. With the price difference, the Forclaz is a much better buy. I can't say how long the bag would last but its been around 6 months and I used it for a few trips and it still looks brand new.

Other notes:
The bag can be cinched down when its not packed full to keep things from moving around. There are sales from Decathlon once in a while so if you can wait, save 10 bucks. The colors for the bags are pretty nice and pretty lowkey, complete opposite of the Cotopaxi.
